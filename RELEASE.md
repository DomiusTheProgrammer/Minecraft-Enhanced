# Minecraft Enhanced — Version 1.0.0

Minecraft Enhanced is a Bedrock Edition add-on with new equipment and vanilla-inspired gameplay improvements.

## Download

Download **`Minecraft Enhanced.mcaddon`** from the Assets section below and open it with Minecraft to import both packs.

After importing, enable **Minecraft Enhanced - BP** and **Minecraft Enhanced - RP** in your world's settings.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock Edition **1.21.120 or newer**
- `@minecraft/server` Script API **2.0.0** (declared by the behavior pack)

## Included

- Steel, quartz, emerald, amethyst, and obsidian tools
- Steel, quartz, emerald, and amethyst armor
- Crafting and smelting recipes for custom equipment and materials
- Additional loot in selected structures
- Tool interactions: strip logs, till soil, make dirt paths, and extinguish campfires
- Resource-pack textures and armor attachables
